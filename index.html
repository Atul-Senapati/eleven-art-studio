Meet the Makers

A story of quiet strength & boundless creativity

11:Eleven Art Studio was born from a mother-daughter duo's shared love for craftsmanship. The mother, a natural homemaker, mastered resin art and baking, evolving into an entrepreneur. Her daughter, a Counselling Psychologist, left ed-tech to build something personal.

Together, they pour intention into every candle and package, celebrating craftsmanship, connection, and conscious gifting.
